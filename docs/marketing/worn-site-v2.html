How it works

The best part of shopping was always the wait.

The scroll, the haul, the moment you hit pay, the notification that says it's nearly here. WORN keeps every one of those moments and swaps the parcel for photos of you in the clothes.

Step 1

Make your avatar

Upload one photo and WORN builds the model you'll be wearing everything on. You can skip it and add one later.

Step 2

Browse today's edit

Real pieces from real stores. Take the style quiz and your feed reorders around your taste. Filter by dresses, tops, bags and more.

Step 3

Check out with coins

Fill your haul and pay with coins. You get 500 when you join. Then pick how long you want to wait for it.

Step 4

Track it home

Watch it go from packed to out for delivery, with a notification at every step. Can't wait? Rush to Express for 25 coins.

Step 5

Open your reveal

Your haul lands as a deck of renders of you wearing it, in real-looking scenes. Swipe through, then share your favourite.

Pick your pace

How long do you want to look forward to it?

Every order moves through five real stages. Express gets there in about two hours. Slow burn takes two days, for people who like it that way.

Questions

The things people ask first.

Does anything actually arrive?

No, and that's the point. You get the whole thrill of shopping, from the haul to the wait to the unboxing, and what arrives is renders of you wearing it. Nothing to pay off, store or send back.

Can I buy the real thing?

Yes. Every piece links to a store that sells it. Buying through WORN's link earns you 25 coins of cashback.

What are coins, and do I have to pay?

Coins are WORN's currency. You get 500 when you join and earn more every day from streaks and referrals. If you want more, coin packs start at ₹99.

What happens to my photo?

It's used to build your avatar so the renders look like you. You can delete your photo and avatar any time with one tap in your profile.

When can I get it?

WORN is heading into beta on iOS and Android. Join the list and we'll tell you when your invite is ready.
